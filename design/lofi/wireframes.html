Mu Katale — central market system: low-fi wireframes

What it is. Mu Katale is an online central market for fresh produce and market commodities in Uganda, together with the back-office system that runs it. Buyers — hotels, schools, restaurants and households — see what is in stock, at what unit price and when it can be delivered, then order, pay by mobile money, wallet or cash on delivery, and follow the order until it arrives. Market administrators and vendors use the staff side of the same system to publish stock, fulfil orders, collect payments, raise purchase orders and read the audit trail.

The problem. Buying market commodities still means physical market visits, phone calls and informal middlemen. Buyers cannot reliably see current availability, unit prices or delivery schedules before they commit, so they over-order to avoid stockouts or under-order and miss requirements. Vendors and administrators work from memory, paper and message threads, which do not enforce order status, audit stock changes or produce the invoices, receipts and delivery records that institutional buyers need.

How these screens answer it.

  • Real-time visibility before ordering: every product shows its price, farm and stock remaining (WF-01, 02, 04).
  • Administrators manage stock and fulfil orders without extra workload: one staff desk for the order queue, catalogue, inventory and purchase orders (WF-23 to 28).
  • Trust without inspecting goods: named farms, a delivery timeline and a clear order status (WF-04, 12, 24).
  • Bulk and small purchases in one interface: one basket and checkout for every buyer, with saved details, order history and preferences on My page (WF-07 to 16).
  • Auditable stock: every movement carries a reason, a reference and an actor (WF-26, 31).

Reading the sheet: all 33 screens of the buyer and staff sides are drawn as grey boxes at 393 × 852, the size the app is built for. Under each frame, what is tappable and where it goes is listed (① tap … → WF-nn).
